02 · The Challenge

Transforming In-Flight Connectivity from a Black Box to Transparent Clarity

Passengers flying across domestic and international routes frequently suffer from opaque satellite Wi-Fi pricing, sluggish captive portals, and zero visibility into satellite coverage handoffs. The FlyFi web redesign was conceived to deliver instant clarity before passengers board and while in transit:

Legacy Web Friction

Fragmented Airline Portals

  • ✕ No Pre-Flight Equipment Transparency: Passengers cannot verify whether their specific aircraft tail number has Starlink, Viasat, or Gogo Ku-band.
  • ✕ Sluggish Captive Portals: Confusing multi-step authentication leads to failed logins and unexpected in-flight charges.
  • ✕ Disjointed Desktop Hierarchy: Outdated travel blogs and clunky tables create poor readability on high-resolution laptop displays.
  • ✕ Terminal Search Friction: Travelers cannot easily look up layover airport Wi-Fi passwords without installing separate heavy apps.
The Redesigned Web Experience

Instant Travel Intelligence

  • ✓ Live 3D Flight Radar Web Portal: Real-time trajectory with altitude profiles, ground speed, and satellite beam handoff zones.
  • ✓ Airline Wi-Fi Database: Transparent speed test benchmarks for Delta, United, Emirates, Qatar Airways, and 35+ carriers.
  • ✓ Airport Lounge & Gate Wi-Fi: Searchable terminal credentials and captive portal bypass guides for 500+ global hubs.
  • ✓ Unified Token Architecture: High-contrast typography and responsive layouts optimized across laptop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

09 · Retrospective

Engineering Retrospective & Design Reflections

Redesigning an aviation web platform that serves both high-stress travelers and aviation enthusiasts yielded critical insights regarding performance, data density, and web-to-app synergy:

Takeaway 01

Tabular Alignment Prevents Eye Fatigue

Flight altitude and speed metrics fluctuate continuously. Utilizing `JetBrains Mono` with fixed-width numerals ensured that altitude profiles never caused layout shifts or visual jitter as data streamed into the browser.

Takeaway 02

High-Contrast Light Surfaces Win in Travel

While dark mode is popular for night flights, daytime travel planning on laptops in bright airport concourses requires crisp white surfaces (#FFFFFF) and 1px distinct borders to resist direct solar glare through terminal windows.

Takeaway 03

Positioning Web as the Acquisition Funnel

Rather than trying to replicate all mobile app features on the desktop, the web experience is optimized for pre-trip research: searching airline Wi-Fi speed benchmarks and directing travelers to install the mobile app for gate notifications.
