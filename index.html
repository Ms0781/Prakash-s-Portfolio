Design Process

How I Craft UI/UX

01. Discovery 02. Wireframing 03. Design System 04. Prototyping 05. Dev Handoff

01. Research & User Journey

Stakeholder interviews, clinical persona mapping, and empathy mapping to uncover real pain points and structure intuitive information architecture.

02. Wireframing & UX Flows

Low-to-high fidelity wireframes and user flow mapping in Figma to test navigation logic, data hierarchy, and remove user friction early.

03. Design System & UI Kit

Building scalable atomic component libraries with Figma Auto Layout, responsive data grids, typography scales, and unified design tokens.

04. Prototyping & Dev Handoff

Interactive clickable prototypes validated with real users, paired with pixel-perfect Figma specs and tokens for 40% faster developer handoff.

Animation & Edit

How I Craft Video Editing & Logo Animation

01. Concept & Storyboard 02. Sound & Rhythm Pacing 03. Keyframe Animation 04. 3D Logo Dynamics 05. Color Grading & 4K Master Ae/Pr Adobe Suite

01. Concept Scripting & Storyboard

Analyzing core brand narrative, establishing emotional pacing, timing visual beats to sonic frequencies, and sketching thumbnail storyboards for seamless frame transitions.

02. Vector Rigging & Kinetic Typography

Deconstructing vector logos into animated sub-paths, setting 3D anchor points, parenting null controllers, and crafting expressive kinetic typography with custom velocity curves.

03. Keyframe Crafting & 3D Logo Dynamics

Sculpting smooth motion curves in After Effects with graph editor easing, realistic optical flares, volumetric lighting, particle simulations, and fluid logo reveals.

04. Sound Design, Color LUTs & 4K Master Export

Multi-track Foley sound effects, sub-bass risers, cinematic color grading in Premiere Pro, and exporting 4K Ultra-HD 60fps deliverables optimized for web, reels, and broadcast.
